224 Clubhouse — Website Owner's Guide

Everything you need to run your website, in plain language. No technical knowledge required.

Welcome

This guide explains how to use your 224 Clubhouse website day to day — adding products, managing orders, running events, and looking after memberships. Keep it handy; think of it as your manual for the whole system.

Your website has two sides:

  1. The public side — what your customers see and use (the shop, membership sign-up, events, contact page). You don't need to do anything here; it runs itself.
  2. The admin side — your private control room. This is where you, the owner, add products, watch orders come in, and manage members. It is protected by a login, so only you and your staff can reach it.

1. Signing in to the control room

  1. Open your web browser and go to https://224clubhouse.web.app/admin/login.
  2. You'll see the 224 logo and a Sign In box.
  3. Type your email address and password (the ones you were given when the site was set up).
  4. Click the gold Sign In button.
  5. If your details are correct, you land on the Dashboard. If not, a red message says "Invalid credentials" — check your typing and try again.

🔒 Only approved staff accounts can get in. Even if someone else knows the web address, they cannot reach your products, orders, or members without an approved login. If you need to add another staff member, contact your website support person (see section 12) — they'll create the account for you.

2. The Dashboard — your daily snapshot

The first screen after you sign in. It gives you the health of the business at a glance.

You'll see four summary cards:

  • Total Products — how many items are in your shop.
  • Total Orders — how many orders you've ever received.
  • Total Revenue — total money taken, shown in Rands.
  • Pending Orders — orders that still need your attention.

Below the cards: Recent Orders — a list of the latest orders so you can see activity at a glance. To work with orders properly, go to the Orders page (section 4).

To move around, use the menu on the left:

3. Products — your shop stock

This is where you control everything customers can buy. Click Products in the left menu.

You'll see a table of all your products with a small picture, the category, the price, how many are in stock, whether it's available, and action buttons.

Adding a new product

  1. Click the gold Add Product button (top right).
  2. A form pops up. Fill it in:
    • Product Name (required) — e.g. "OG Kush".
    • Description — a few sentences about the product (optional but recommended).
    • Price (Rands) (required) — type the price in Rands, e.g. 50.00.
    • Stock Quantity (required) — how many you have, e.g. 100.
    • Category (required) — choose Flower, Edibles, Accessories, or Merchandise.
  3. If you chose "Flower", three extra boxes appear:
    • Strain Type — Indica, Sativa, or Hybrid.
    • THC % — e.g. 25.0.
    • Weight (g) — e.g. 3.5.
  4. Two switches:
    • Available — leave ON so customers can buy it. Turn OFF to hide it from the shop.
    • Members Only — turn ON if only members should see/buy this item.
  5. Add pictures:
    • Click the dashed upload box (with the up-arrow icon).
    • Choose one or more photos from your computer or phone. You can select several at once.
    • They appear as small thumbnails. To remove one, click the little ✕ on its corner.
    • The first picture is the one customers see first, so put your best shot first.
  6. Click the gold Add Product button at the bottom.
  7. A green message confirms "Product created" and your item appears in the list.

Editing a product

  1. Find the product in the list.
  2. Click the pencil icon in its row.
  3. The same form opens, already filled in. Change whatever you need.
  4. Click Update Product. A green "Product updated" message confirms it.

Turning a product on or off (quickest way to hide/show)

In the Available column, click the toggle switch:

  • Green (pointing right) = customers can see and buy it.
  • Grey (pointing left) = hidden from the shop.

This is the fastest way to temporarily pull something from sale (e.g. you've run out) without deleting it. It changes instantly.

Deleting a product

  1. Click the trash-can icon in the product's row.
  2. A box asks "Delete this product? This cannot be undone."
  3. Click OK to confirm. A message says "Product deleted."

⚠️ Deleting is permanent. If you only want to stop selling something for a while, use the Available toggle instead — that way you keep the product and its photos for later.

4. Orders — sales coming in

Every time a customer pays, an order lands here. Click Orders in the left menu.

You'll see a table with the order, the customer's name and email, the total, the current status, and a dropdown to change the status.

Filtering orders

At the top is a row of buttons: all, pending, paid, processing, shipped, delivered, cancelled. Click one to show only orders in that state. Click all to see everything. This helps you focus — for example, click paid to see orders you still need to prepare.

Seeing the full details of an order

Click the arrow (chevron) at the end of an order's row. It expands to show:

  • The delivery address and phone number.
  • The exact items ordered, with quantities and prices.
  • The payment reference number.

Click the arrow again to close it.

Updating an order's status

As you prepare and send an order, keep its status up to date so you always know where things stand:

  1. Find the order.
  2. Click the status dropdown.
  3. Choose the new status. A typical journey is: paid → processing → shipped → delivered.
  4. A message confirms "Order status updated."
StatusWhat it means
pendingOrder started but payment not completed
paidCustomer has paid — ready for you to prepare
processingYou're packing/preparing it
shippedIt's on its way
deliveredCustomer has received it
cancelledOrder was cancelled

5. Events — what's on

Promote your sessions, parties, and gatherings. Click Events in the left menu. You'll see a list of all events with a picture, date and time, whether it's members-only, and the ticket price.

Adding an event

  1. Click Add Event (top right).
  2. Fill in the form:
    • Event Title (required) — e.g. "Sesh Sunday".
    • Description — what it's about.
    • Date (required) — pick the day.
    • Time — pick the start time (optional).
    • Location — already filled with your address; change it if the event is elsewhere.
    • Ticket Price (Rands) — type a price, or leave it blank to make the event free.
  3. Upload an image — click the upload link and choose a photo (one image per event).
  4. Members Only switch — turn ON if it's for members only.
  5. Click Add Event. A confirmation appears and it shows in the list.

Editing or deleting an event

  • Edit: click the pencil icon, change the details, click Update Event.
  • Delete: click the trash-can icon, confirm, and it's removed.

6. Memberships — your members

Manage everyone who has signed up to join the club. Click Memberships in the left menu.

At the top, four summary cards:

  • Total Applications — everyone who has applied.
  • Active Members — currently valid memberships.
  • Pending Review — applications waiting for your decision.
  • Active Revenue — money from current memberships.

A search box lets you find someone fast — start typing a name or email and the list filters instantly.

The members table shows each person's name, email, phone, their tier (Daily Pass, Weekly, or Monthly), their status, the amount they paid, and when their membership expires.

Changing a member's status

In each member's row there are small action buttons:

  • ✅ Green tick — mark the member Active (approved and current).
  • ❌ Red cross — Cancel the membership.
  • 🕐 Grey clock — mark the membership Expired.

Click the one you want; a short message confirms the change.

What the status colours mean:

ColourStatusMeaning
🟢 GreenActiveMembership is current and valid
🟡 YellowPendingWaiting for you to approve
⚪ GreyExpiredThe membership period has ended
🔴 RedCancelledThe member or you cancelled it

💡 Membership lengths: Daily Pass = 1 day, Weekly = 7 days, Monthly = 30 days. The expiry date is worked out automatically from when they joined.

7. Signing out

When you're finished, always sign out — especially on a shared or public computer.

  • Click Sign Out at the bottom of the left menu.
  • You'll return to the login screen. Your control room is locked again.

8. What your customers experience

You don't manage this part — it runs automatically — but it helps to know what your customers go through.

Buying a product:

  1. Age check — first-time visitors must confirm they are 21 or older before the site opens. (Visitors who say they're under 21 are sent away.)
  2. The Store — they browse products, search by name, and filter by category.
  3. Product page — they view photos (with a gallery), read the details, pick a quantity, and click Add to Cart.
  4. Cart — they review their items and the total. Orders over a set amount get free shipping; otherwise a delivery fee is added.
  5. Checkout — they enter their name, contact details and delivery address, tick the 21+ confirmation, and pay securely by card through Paystack.
  6. Confirmation — they see an order confirmation, and the order appears in your Orders page.

Joining as a member:

  1. On the Membership page they choose a tier (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
  2. They fill in their details and date of birth, and agree to the 12 Club Commandments.
  3. They pay through Paystack.
  4. They see a welcome screen and are asked to present their confirmation at the door. The new member then appears in your Memberships page.

Other pages customers can visit: Events (what's on), About (your story), and Contact (your address, phone, hours, a map, and a message form).

💳 About payments: All card payments are handled securely by Paystack. Your website never sees or stores customers' card numbers.

9. Everyday tasks — quick recipes

"I've run out of an item."
→ Products → click the green Available toggle so it turns grey. The item disappears from the shop until you switch it back on. (No need to delete it.)

"New stock arrived."
→ Products → pencil icon on the item → update the Stock Quantity → if it was hidden, turn Available back on → Update Product.

"I want to add this week's special."
→ Products → Add Product → fill in the details and photos → Add Product.

"A customer paid — what now?"
→ Orders → find the order (filter by paid) → open it with the arrow to see the address and items → prepare it → set status to processing, then shipped, then delivered as you go.

"I'm promoting Friday's event."
→ Events → Add Event → title, date, time, price (or leave blank for free), photo → Add Event.

"Approve a new member."
→ Memberships → find them (use search) → click the green tick to mark them Active.

10. Good habits & housekeeping

  • Use good photos. Clear, well-lit, square-ish pictures look best. The first photo is the main one customers see.
  • Keep stock numbers honest. Update quantities when stock changes so the shop never sells something you don't have.
  • Process orders promptly and keep their status current — it's how you track what still needs doing.
  • Prefer the Available toggle over Delete. Toggling hides an item but keeps it (and its photos) for next time. Deleting is permanent.
  • Always sign out on shared computers.
  • Use a strong password for your admin login and don't share it.
  • The admin address is private. Treat it like the keys to your shop.

11. Common questions & troubleshooting

I can't log in.
Double-check your email and password (watch for capital letters and spaces). If it still won't work, ask your support contact (section 12) to reset your password.

The age check keeps appearing / a customer says they can't get past it.
The 21+ confirmation is remembered per device and per browser. It will show again on a new phone or computer, or if someone clears their browser history. That's normal.

I added a product but customers can't see it.
Check two things: the Available switch is green (on), and Members Only is off if it should be for everyone.

My new product has no picture in the shop.
You probably didn't upload a photo, or it was still uploading when you saved. Edit the product and add an image. While images upload you'll see "Uploading…" — wait for the thumbnail to appear before saving.

A price looks wrong.
Always type prices in Rands (e.g. 50.00), not cents. Edit the product to fix it.

An order is stuck on "pending".
"Pending" usually means the customer started but didn't finish paying. You only need to act on orders marked paid.

A customer says they paid but didn't get a confirmation email.
Check the Orders page — if the order is there and marked paid, you have their details and can contact them directly. If confirmation emails aren't arriving at all, tell your support contact so they can check the email setup.

Something looks broken or won't load.
Try refreshing the page first. If it persists, note what you were doing and contact support (section 12).
